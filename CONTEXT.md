# Portfolio

A personal portfolio site presenting Alaa Faraj's engineering work to people deciding
whether to interview him. It exists to carry a visitor from a short skim to one of two
actions: downloading the CV, or sending an email.

## Language

### Audience

**Screener**:
The primary visitor: a recruiter or internship reviewer skimming for roughly thirty
seconds to decide whether this candidate is worth a closer look.
_Avoid_: user, visitor, recruiter

**Engineer**:
The secondary visitor: a technical reader who follows GitHub links and reads the work
itself rather than the summary.
_Avoid_: developer, technical user

### Work

**Project**:
Self-directed work that was built and shipped, owned end to end rather than assigned.
_Avoid_: app, repo, portfolio piece

**Experience**:
A role held at an organisation, paid or as a structured placement, in which real work
was shipped. A programme that involves doing the work is Experience, not a Credential.
_Avoid_: job, internship, training

**Coursework**:
Work produced to satisfy a graded assignment or a course module. Real output, but
scoped by someone else's brief.
_Avoid_: project, assignment, homework

**Credential**:
Evidence of a completed programme of study issued by an outside body, whether that is
a certificate, a nanodegree, or a course completion.
_Avoid_: certification, certificate, training

### Documents

**CV**:
The single canonical PDF résumé offered for download. There is exactly one; any other
copy is stale.
_Avoid_: resume, Final_CV
